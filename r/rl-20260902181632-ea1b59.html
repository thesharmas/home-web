So how big *was* Bessent's yentervention?

FT Alphaville investigates the actual size of the US Treasury's July 2025 intervention in the Japanese yen market. After reviewing ESF financial statements and weekly foreign exchange reserve releases, the author estimates the intervention was approximately $500mn — far smaller than the $5-10bn clue Scott Bessent appeared to plant for journalists and a fraction of the theoretical $26.3bn maximum. Former US Treasury deputy assistant secretary Brad Setser independently arrived at a similar figure.
