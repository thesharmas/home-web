Invisible Companies

The essay introduces the concept of 'invisible companies'—businesses that earn sustained above-normal profits not because of traditional moats like patents or scale, but because competitors simply fail to notice them. Drawing on examples like Steve Ross's Kinney Services, Constellation Software, HEICO, and Waste Management, the authors argue that competitive neglect—driven by unawareness, missing data, cognitive filters, and status bias—is itself a durable source of excess returns.
