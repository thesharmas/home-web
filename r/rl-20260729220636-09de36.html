Controlling Reasoning Effort in LLMs

The article explains how modern LLMs implement multiple reasoning effort modes (e.g., low, medium, high) by examining training techniques such as effort-conditioned RLVR with variable token-length penalties and supervised fine-tuning with mixed thinking/non-thinking examples. It surveys six open-weight flagship models—DeepSeek V4, Nemotron 3 Ultra, Kimi K2.5/K3, GLM-5, Qwen3, and Inkling—detailing how each trains and exposes reasoning effort controls.
