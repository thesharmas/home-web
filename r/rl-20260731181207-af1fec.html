BNP Paribas: running from Sudan

BNP Paribas lost a first-instance civil lawsuit brought by Darfur genocide victims over its role in financing Sudan in violation of sanctions, for which it previously pleaded guilty and paid an $8.9 billion fine. On appeal, BNPP has marshalled an unusually large array of amicus briefs including from the US government (filed by SDNY US Attorney Jay Clayton, formerly a partner at Sullivan & Cromwell which represented BNPP in its 2014 DOJ settlement) and the Swiss government (147 pages).
