Why your snacks are chunkier and chewier than before

Big food companies like Mondelez and Mars are increasingly competing on texture—crunch, chewiness, and mouthfeel—rather than novel flavours alone. Demand for texture-indulgent snacks in America jumped 28% in two years, driven by GLP-1 drug users seeking more satisfying experiences and social-media trends like "crunchtok." Manufacturers view texture as the last major frontier for differentiation now that flavour innovation has largely plateaued.
