The confounding 'compounding' of 'compounding'

The article examines the overuse of the word "compounding" in corporate earnings calls and executive commentary, noting that the term is frequently misapplied as a vague synonym for growth. After explaining the genuine financial meaning of compounding — where subsequent change occurs to an already-changed entity, driven by reinvestment — the author collects examples from executives at Sysco, Spotify, Palantir, NatWest, and others all using the word in the same week.
