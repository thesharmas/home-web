Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness seeks to raise capital after AI rout

Situational Awareness, the $20bn AI-focused hedge fund founded by former OpenAI employee Leopold Aschenbrenner, has sought fresh capital from investors after suffering heavy losses during a recent rout in AI stocks. The fund had posted extraordinary returns of 439% net in the first half of the year, but its use of leverage to amplify bets on AI-linked companies has magnified losses as tech stocks, particularly in Asia and the Nasdaq, sold off sharply.
