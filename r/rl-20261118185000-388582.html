SoftBank Just Paid $3.7 Billion to Walk Away From an Estimated 4GW of Gas Turbines

SoftBank wrote off $3.7 billion in non-refundable deposits and contract settlements on gas turbine reservations it no longer needs for U.S. AI data center projects, with one analyst estimating roughly 4 GW of cancellations from an original 13+ GW position held mostly with GE Vernova. Despite the writedown, secondary turbine markets remain tight and prices elevated, suggesting SoftBank may be an isolated case rather than the start of a broad wave of cancellations—though GE Vernova's stock is down ~35% from its high as public markets price in project slippage.
