Already Clean: Neural Networks Are More Interpretable Than We Thought

The author argues that neural networks are far more interpretable than previously believed, challenging the dominant "superposition" hypothesis that networks arbitrarily pack unrelated concepts into neurons when space runs out. Instead, they propose that most apparent polysemanticity is explained by "recursive generalization" — neurons branching into related adjacent concepts during training, much like a tree growing sub-branches.
