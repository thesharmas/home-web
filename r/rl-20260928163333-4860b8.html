What is the AI capex breakeven rate?

Robin Wigglesworth examines how much AI revenue the major hyperscalers (Amazon, Alphabet, Microsoft) need to generate to justify their massive data centre capital expenditure, drawing on estimates from Goldman Sachs, Columbia Business School, and other economists. Goldman Sachs estimates hyperscalers need ~$300bn in annual AI revenues just to break even, and ~$636bn for a 30% return on invested capital—roughly 10x current run rates. Multiple analyses converge on the view that AI revenues must grow 13–45x over six years for the investment plans to make economic sense.
