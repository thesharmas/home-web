What the increasingly awkward data centre construction delays mean for investors

Data centre construction delays are creating significant credit risks for bond investors, with S&P Global counting 146 delayed or cancelled projects, three-quarters tied to local community opposition. Key risks include tenant termination rights triggered by milestone delays, inconsistent force majeure clauses across deal documents, and the potential for a future compute oversupply that could leave creditors exposed. Investors are scrutinising complex, idiosyncratic deal structures to understand who bears the losses if construction timelines slip too far.
