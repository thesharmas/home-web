Will AI soon lead to double-digit growth?

Ben Moll and Alex Imas argue that despite being bullish on AI capabilities, predictions of double-digit GDP growth (10-30%+ per year) within the next 10-15 years are extremely unlikely. They show that while standard growth models can theoretically produce explosive AI-driven growth, five key assumptions must all hold simultaneously: fast economy-wide automation, continued spending on automated goods, sufficient demand and investment, no AI-driven cyber destruction of value, and AI-accelerated R&D.
