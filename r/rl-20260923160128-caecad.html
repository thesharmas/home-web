The argument for Kalshi taking bets on its own legality

Stanford Law professor Joseph Grundfest has proposed that prediction market platform Kalshi list a contract allowing bets on the outcome of US Supreme Court litigation over whether Kalshi's sports-indexed contracts are legal. Grundfest argues such a contract would offer substantial hedging value for Kalshi's investors and customers, given that sports betting accounts for roughly 90-95% of Kalshi's revenue and the company is seeking a $40bn valuation. Kalshi declined, saying it does not list markets on itself, citing concerns that its own legal team could influence the outcome.
