A half-dozen Halloween charts to haunt and horrify investors

M&G fixed income director Joe Sullivan-Bissett presents six Halloween-themed charts highlighting risks for investors: a widening gap between US consumer spending and income growth, a quadrupling of diesel refining premiums, a near-frozen US housing market, historically tight credit spreads, a debt-fuelled hyperscaler capex boom, and France's government bond yields rising above former periphery countries like Greece and Portugal.
