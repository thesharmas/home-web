CoreWeave's bonds scream financial distress

CoreWeave's bonds are now trading at distressed levels, with credit spreads exceeding 1000 basis points over government bonds. The cost of insuring $10mn of CoreWeave debt against default has reached $1.1mn per annum, and CDS contracts have moved to upfront pricing, signalling serious market concern. The piece contextualises these developments against CoreWeave co-founder Brannin McBee's stated strategy of tapping insurance capital by securing investment-grade ratings on various forms of debt.
