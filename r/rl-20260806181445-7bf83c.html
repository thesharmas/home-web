SoftBank Borrows Against OpenAI Stake as It Invests More

SoftBank borrowed $10 billion against its OpenAI stake, just a month after investing another $10 billion into the company. The move highlights the financial risks SoftBank is taking with its massive OpenAI investment, having already deployed $20 billion of a planned $30 billion second investment on top of an earlier $34 billion commitment. The loan-against-stake maneuver signals the Japanese investment giant is stretching its finances to maintain and grow its OpenAI position.
