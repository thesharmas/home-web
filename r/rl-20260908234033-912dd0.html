A suspicious signal in a dark-matter detector has physicists excited

A mysterious signal detected on June 16th 2023 in the LUX-ZEPLIN (LZ) underground dark-matter experiment in South Dakota has excited physicists. The experiment, part of the ongoing hunt for WIMPs (Weakly Interacting Massive Particles), registered an anomalous blip that could potentially be significant. Researchers are cautiously optimistic, acknowledging it may be nothing or could represent a major breakthrough in dark matter detection.
