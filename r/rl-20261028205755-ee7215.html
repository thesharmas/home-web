We don't understand the gold price

The article explores the historically inverse relationship between gold prices and real US Treasury yields (TIPS), noting how that relationship broke down after Russia's invasion of Ukraine in 2022. Various explanations for gold's subsequent rally — central bank buying, sovereign wealth funds, China, Tether, preppers — are canvassed without firm conclusion. The author observes that while the relationship partially reasserted itself in early 2026, gold has again decoupled from TIPS yields in a confounding way, moving lower even as bond yields rally.
