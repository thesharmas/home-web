A controversial new power grab in Pakistan

Pakistan's de facto ruler, Field Marshal Asim Munir, is pursuing a plan to break the country's four provinces into smaller units, a move critics see as an attempt to weaken dynastic political parties like the Sharifs' PML-N and the Bhutto family's PPP rather than a genuine governance reform. The proposal has sparked fierce resistance, particularly from Sindh, whose assembly passed a resolution rejecting it, while a national referendum is rumoured as a way to bypass constitutional requirements for provincial assembly approval.
