SpaceX's staggered lock-up release prolongs the pain

SpaceX adopted a staggered lock-up release schedule after its record $86.25bn IPO, spreading insider share sales across multiple tranches tied to earnings releases and price targets rather than a single 180-day cliff-edge expiry. The author argues this approach, modelled partly on Cerebras's IPO structure, has not reassured investors — short interest has soared to 34% of the float and the stock has fallen to roughly half its all-time high.
