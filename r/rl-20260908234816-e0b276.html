Europe's bond markets are suffering a post-holiday shock

The Economist reports that Europe's bond markets are experiencing turbulence following the summer holiday period, with rising yields presenting challenges for investors. Euro-zone government bond investors must navigate 21 different country markets plus EU debt, compounding the difficulty. The article notes that while the reasons for rising yields differ from those in America, they are equally problematic.
