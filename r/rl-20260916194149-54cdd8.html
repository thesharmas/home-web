Money Talks: The Zero-Sum Mistake

Roy Swan, director of the Ford Foundation's mission investments program, joins Elizabeth Spiers on Slate's Money Talks podcast to challenge the zero-sum view of the economy. Swan argues that the belief that someone must lose for another to win is both socially and economically costly, and that positive impact and positive financial returns are not mutually exclusive. The discussion ties to Swan's new book, 'Positive Sum: How Zero-sum Thinking Broke Capitalism—and How We Can Fix It.'
