The Situational Awareness Fund Blow-up: Collateral Damage from Investment Conviction!

Aswath Damodaran uses the rise and fall of Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness hedge fund—which gained ~450% before losing over two-thirds of its public equity value in four weeks—as a lens to examine investment conviction: what it is, where it comes from, and how it drives portfolio concentration and leverage decisions. He argues that excessive conviction, amplified by heavy leverage and a concentrated macro AI bet, turned a bad month into a fund-ending event, and that 'humble money' consistently outperforms 'smart money' over time.
