Zuckerberg v. Amodei in AI Marketing

Martin Peers contrasts how Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg and Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei are publicly marketing their AI visions: Zuckerberg via lengthy optimistic essays emphasizing AI's democratizing potential, while Amodei leans into AI's dangers and risks. The piece also covers a surge in AI infrastructure financing, including a Macquarie/GIC data center venture for Anthropic and a $500 billion Nvidia partnership with major Wall Street firms. Brief mentions of Intel's $15 billion stock offering, Monday.com earnings, and a Sony/TSMC image sensor joint venture round out the briefing.
