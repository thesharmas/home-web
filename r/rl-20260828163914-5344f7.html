Harvard and the Cloudflare governance fight

Cloudflare founders Matt Prince and Michelle Zatlyn, both Harvard Business School alumni, are embroiled in a major corporate governance dispute over their attempt to create new share classes to preserve their voting control of the company despite a dwindling economic stake. Shareholder plaintiffs are challenging the independence of the special committee that approved the restructuring, citing close personal and professional ties between the directors and the founders, including a professor who stayed at Prince's home and used an AI chatbot to build a one-sided case for founder control.
