Tata goes to war with itself

A boardroom clash at Tata Sons has erupted between Noel Tata, chair of Tata Trusts (the conglomerate's largest shareholder), and Natarajan Chandrasekaran, chair of Tata Sons, after the board voted 4-1 to give Chandrasekaran a third five-year term and to cease resisting an RBI mandate to list the company. Noel Tata has declared the vote a "legal nullity" and both sides have lawyered up, with the dispute heading to court over what constitutes a majority under Tata's articles of association.
