The coolest use for the Vision Pro

Christian Selig describes using the Apple Vision Pro to explore 3D models of a house he is building, converting floor plans into immersive walkthroughs using Fusion 360, IKEA 3D models, and 3D Warehouse assets. He vibe-coded a custom Vision Pro app called Prospector with controller support, terrain following, and other features to make navigating the virtual space practical. He argues this workflow makes spatial decision-making during home design far more intuitive than reviewing 2D floor plan PDFs.
