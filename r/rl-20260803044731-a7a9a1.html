Retail investors should beware perpetual futures

The article explains perpetual futures ("perps"), a derivative product invented by BitMEX's Arthur Hayes in 2016 that removes the expiry date from traditional futures contracts. It details how a built-in funding rate effectively transfers roughly 10% per year from retail longs to professional shorts, making perps a lucrative product for exchanges and sophisticated traders at the expense of ordinary punters. Once confined to crypto, perps are now spreading to mainstream assets like stocks and commodities and are becoming accessible to US retail investors onshore.
