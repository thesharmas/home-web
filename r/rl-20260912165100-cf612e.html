India's payments system is ten. It must start paying for itself

India's Unified Payments Interface (UPI) marks its tenth anniversary processing 25 billion transactions in August 2026 alone, having transformed the country from a cash-dominant economy to a largely digital one. The system's success has been driven by its simplicity, cheap mobile data, and a government ban on merchant transaction fees since 2020. However, new legislation re-legalising fees signals a reckoning: banks are under-subsidised and under-invested, and UPI must find a sustainable funding model to avoid stagnation.
