Brady's Lessons

The article examines the Brady Plan of 1989 as a model for resolving today's sovereign debt crises in developing nations, reviewing how it succeeded where the Baker Plan failed by cutting debt stocks and coordinating multilateral institutions. It analyzes the shortcomings of the Common Framework, the current debt-restructuring mechanism, through case studies of Chad, Zambia, Ghana, and Ethiopia.
