How nervous are bond markets about France?

The article examines rising French government bond spreads, using cross-currency asset swaps to compare yields on an apples-to-apples basis across major economies. While France's spreads have widened and look worse than peers on a swap-adjusted basis, they remain far below the levels seen during the Eurozone Crisis. The author argues that bond vigilantes have limited near-term power over France given ECB rate dynamics and the country's economic weight in the Eurozone.
