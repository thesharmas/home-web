Making sense of George Lucas's new billion-dollar galaxy

The Lucas Museum of Narrative Art, a $1bn, 300,000-square-foot institution in Los Angeles founded by George Lucas, opened on September 22nd 2026. The museum focuses on "narrative art" and displays Lucas's personal collection—including Star Wars props, illustrations, and old masters—alongside digital reproductions of works held elsewhere, but has attracted criticism for its near-total absence of wall text and curatorial explanation.
