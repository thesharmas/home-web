The Almighty Dollar: 500 Years of the World's Most Powerful Money — Brendan Greeley

Financial journalist Brendan Greeley traces the global history of the dollar back 500 years to its roots in 16th-century silver mines in Joachimsthal, arguing the dollar pre-dates the United States and following its journey through the Spanish Empire, colonial America, and 19th-century banking panics to its role as the world's primary reserve currency.
