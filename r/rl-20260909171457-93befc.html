The clock is ticking on Senegal's 'total return swaps'

Senegal's debt crisis has deepened following credit downgrades by S&P and Moody's, which may trigger an accelerated termination clause in a €300mn total return swap with First Abu Dhabi Bank, potentially forcing Senegal to repay the full amount within two days. The swaps, which involve international banks lending hard currency against domestic bond collateral, blur the line between external and domestic debt, complicating any restructuring negotiations.
