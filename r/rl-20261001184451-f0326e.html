Everything in Markets Is Now Moving Incredibly Fast

Bloomberg's Odd Lots podcast features Luke Kawa, head of markets at Sherwood News, discussing the contradictory signals in current markets: surging indices alongside falling individual stocks, rising rates despite a robust economy, AI-driven capex, the war in Iran, and persistent inflation. Kawa argues the single most defining characteristic of this moment is the sheer speed of market moves and the pace of news flow. The episode covers major market themes including the Federal Reserve's ongoing tightening cycle.
