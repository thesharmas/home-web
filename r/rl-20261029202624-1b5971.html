What Wall Street Really Thinks About the AI Trade Now

This Bloomberg Odd Lots podcast episode examines Wall Street's current sentiment on the AI trade following market volatility tied to the Anthropic S-1 and hyperscaler earnings. Alexander 'Alty' Altmann, global head of equities tactical strategies at Barclays, discusses how investors are processing AI's apparent immunity to normal economic laws. Topics include the KOSPI-fication of the S&P 500, historical precedents for AI-related capex, and whether AI is truly insulated from interest rate pressures.
