Thinking Machines Cofounder to Return to OpenAI

Lilian Weng, a cofounder of Thinking Machines Lab, is rejoining OpenAI to work on recursive self-improvement research after departing the startup citing health concerns from stress and workload. Her exit makes her the fourth Thinking Machines cofounder to leave in the past year, leaving Mira Murati and John Schulman as the two remaining cofounders. Thinking Machines, which recently launched its first open-source AI model Inkling, has raised $2 billion at a $10 billion valuation with investors including Nvidia and Andreessen Horowitz.
