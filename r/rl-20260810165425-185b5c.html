Guess What? Tony P Hates You, Too.

A long profile of Anthony Polcari, a DC-based Instagram influencer known as Tony P, who went viral in 2023 posting mundane day-in-the-life content and made roughly half a million dollars in his first two years before his follower count and income declined. The piece explores how hate-watching and online mockery paradoxically fueled his brand deals, the psychological toll of public ridicule, and his pivot toward television broadcasting. With his social-media moment fading, Tony is chasing a bigger career on TV while grappling with the tension between needing his haters and resenting them.
