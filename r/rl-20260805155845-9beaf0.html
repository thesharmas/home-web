A Paywall Is a Salary, Not a Donation

The author argues that paywalls for independent writers represent fair compensation for a professional service, not charity or moral failing. Using comparisons to professional athletes like Donovan Mitchell, she challenges the double standard that treats writing as a hobby that should be free while accepting high salaries in other fields. She also critiques the MLM-style "get rich on Substack" grift that has led some to view the platform as failing when it never delivered on unrealistic promises.
