Remove the Legend

Eugene Wei recounts his formative experience as Amazon's first strategic planning analyst in 1997, producing a monthly 'Analytics Package' of business charts under grueling conditions with Excel and unreliable copy machines. Inspired by Edward Tufte's 'The Visual Display of Quantitative Information,' he argues that line graphs are vastly improved by removing the legend and labeling data series directly on the plot. The post walks through practical Excel chart-editing steps and broader Tuftean principles for honest, efficient data visualization.
