Machine god metaphors eat your brain

Henry Farrell argues that the "machine god" metaphor dominating AI policy debate—rooted in rationalist/singularitarian thought from figures like Yudkowsky and Land—dangerously narrows the conversation to a single question of alignment with a coming superintelligence. He contends that alternative frameworks, particularly viewing AI as a social and cultural technology (akin to Herbert Simon's bounded rationality), reveal richer and more actionable questions about institutions, incentives, and organizational design.
