A Historic El Niño Is Coming That Could Cost...

This Bloomberg Odd Lots podcast episode examines an emerging El Niño event forecasted to be record-breaking in intensity. The hosts speak with Justin Mankin, a geography professor at Dartmouth and director of the Climate Modeling and Impacts Group, about how El Niños are forecasted and why they can cause large, long-lasting economic damage through droughts, flooding, and other catastrophes.
