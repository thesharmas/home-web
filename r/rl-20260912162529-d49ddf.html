A prawn superpower rises

Ecuador has become the world's leading exporter of prawns, surpassing India in 2020 and now accounting for 6% of GDP, driven by a tech-intensive, low-density aquaculture model that achieves ~95% survival rates. After a devastating white-spot disease outbreak around 2000, Ecuador took a different path from Asian rivals by breeding disease-resistant larvae, eliminating antibiotics, and deploying advanced feeding and monitoring technology.
