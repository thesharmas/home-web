Nvidia's $200bn 'balance sheet-as-a-service'

Morgan Stanley's credit team initiates coverage of Nvidia with a 'neutral' rating, coining the term 'balance-sheet-as-a-service' to describe how Nvidia uses its financial strength to subsidise AI infrastructure build-outs for customers. The analysts estimate Nvidia's all-in credit exposure could reach approximately $200bn by end-2028, largely driven by contingent obligations from customer revenue backstops and ecosystem financing structures that are opaque and potentially off-balance-sheet.
