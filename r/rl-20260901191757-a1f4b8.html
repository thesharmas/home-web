Adam Posen Thinks Things Could Get Very Messy for the Fed

Adam Posen, president of the Peterson Institute for International Economics, joins the Odd Lots podcast to discuss the challenges facing the Federal Reserve under new chair Kevin Warsh following his hawkish Jackson Hole speech. Posen grades Warsh's speech and examines questions around rate hikes, inflation dynamics unique to the US, and the Treasury's influence on Fed independence. The conversation also touches on the impact of various Fed task forces and the 'messy jobs' theory of AI.
