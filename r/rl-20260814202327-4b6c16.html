Trucking Is Booming Again and Drivers Aren't Happy About It

Bloomberg's Odd Lots podcast examines the current trucking boom, exploring why freight volumes and billing are up while supply is constrained by regulatory crackdowns on who can drive. The episode features Reed Loustalot, CMO of Truck Parking Club, discussing driver dissatisfaction, the rise of surveillance in the industry, and how a recent Supreme Court ruling could further limit capacity. The conversation questions whether this boom is structurally different from past cycles.
