Harness Engineering – Paper Collection

This curated collection of 21 papers traces the evolution of AI 'harnesses'—everything surrounding model weights that determines how a model interacts with the world—from a bare sampling loop in 2019 to self-modifying harnesses in 2026. Organized into sections covering static harnesses, learnable harnesses, and shipped 2026 systems, it argues that the same model weights can score anywhere from 30% to 95.5% on a benchmark depending solely on harness design. The collection was compiled from a YC Paper Club session recorded August 26, 2026.
