What is Jane Street?

The article explores whether Jane Street is best classified as a proprietary trading firm or a hedge fund, using four criteria: open-ended fund structure, source of leverage, overnight position-holding, and ability to compound returns. The author argues the semantic debate is useful because it sharpens intuitions about systemic risk, noting that traditional prop trading firms are intrinsically limited in the damage they can do to the broader financial system. The key question posed is: if Jane Street blew up, what would it take with it?
