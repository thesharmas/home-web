Either Meta Glasses are selling extremely well or this Citi survey might have some issues

FT Alphaville's Louis Ashworth dissects a Citi consumer electronics survey that claimed 35% of respondents globally own Meta Glasses, a figure that would imply hundreds of millions of owners — far exceeding EssilorLuxottica's reported sales of ~9 million units. After repeatedly pressing Citi for methodological clarification, the author finds the survey used a narrow opt-in cohort of device owners, reported figures as a subset of a subset without clearly labelling them, and ultimately justified the numbers as directional trend indicators rather than absolute penetration rates.
