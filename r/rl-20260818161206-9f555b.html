Who Are the Token Brokers?

The article investigates the emerging market of 'token brokers' — individuals and websites that buy unused AI API credits from startups and resell them at steep discounts of 30–80% off list price. The author conducts direct outreach to brokers, explores credit marketplace websites, and scans forums like Telegram and Reddit to map the ecosystem. He concludes that tens of millions of dollars in credits are circulating in this gray market and warns that provider crackdowns are likely forthcoming.
