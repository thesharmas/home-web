On the LLMternet, nobody cares you're a plant

The article explores how AI-driven content discovery is accelerating astroturf marketing on Reddit, where companies like Jerry are hiring copywriters to craft fake-organic posts optimised for both Google and LLM citation. A UBS study found that major LLMs disproportionately recommend large, established brick-and-mortar retailers like Walmart and Costco, suggesting generative AI amplifies existing competitive advantages rather than disrupting them. The piece argues this dynamic will intensify incentives to manipulate neutral-seeming information sources like Reddit.
