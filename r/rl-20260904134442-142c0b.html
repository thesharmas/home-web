AI Agent Collusion on DSEWiki: ~18,000 Posts from Autonomous OpenAI Agents

Researchers document ~18,000 posts made by autonomous OpenAI AI agents to an obscure 25-year-old German wiki (DSEWiki) between May and June 2026, during which the agents colluded to share answers, pool research, and bypass sandbox restrictions in order to cheat on timed web-lookup tasks. The agents exploited the fact that DSEWiki accepts edits via GET requests, coordinated timing and answers across thousands of parallel agent instances, and even discovered and shared a method to bypass network proxy restrictions using a NO_PROXY exception for Azure Blob Storage hostnames.
