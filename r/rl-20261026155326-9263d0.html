Jaguar's new car is its last shot at survival

Jaguar has unveiled the Type 01, a 1,000-horsepower electric saloon priced from £130,000, representing a radical reinvention of the brand as an all-electric luxury marque after halting production of its existing lineup in 2024. JLR has spent at least £2.25bn on the relaunch, but faces headwinds including uncertain demand for high-end EVs, stiff competition, brand overlap with Range Rover, and a sharp drop in company profits. If the Type 01 fails to attract enough buyers, it may mark the end of the Jaguar brand entirely.
