Fancy a tour through the Middle East?

A review of Simon Sebag Montefiore's "The Cauldron", a sweeping history of the Middle East since 1900 covering Turkey, Iran, Israel, and the Arab world. The reviewer praises its broad scope and human-centered storytelling, noting its rare inclusion of the entire region rather than isolated states, while acknowledging that such breadth sacrifices depth on individual events. A key highlight is the book's treatment of the Iranian revolution as a world-historical turning point comparable to the French and Russian revolutions.
