The Crimes of 1873

This passage describes the devastating impact of the Panic of 1873 on U.S. railroad companies, with railroad stocks falling nearly 50% and a third of railway bonds going into receivership between 1874 and 1876—the worst corporate default period in American history, surpassing even the Great Depression. By 1879, more than half of U.S. railway bonds had stopped paying interest, halting new railroad construction and shuttering half of iron furnaces.
