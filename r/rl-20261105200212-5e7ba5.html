James Bosworth on What Comes After Lula's Stunning Win

James Bosworth, a non-resident fellow at the Stimson Center and author of the Latin America Risk Report newsletter, returns to Bloomberg's Odd Lots podcast to discuss Lula da Silva's surprise presidential victory in Brazil over Flavio Bolsonaro. The episode also covers opposition leader María Corina Machado's return to Venezuela and what these developments mean for the so-called 'orange wave' that had been realigning Latin America toward Trump-era politics. Bosworth looks ahead to 2027 and how it may further reshape the region's political landscape.
