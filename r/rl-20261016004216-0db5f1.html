Large Lending Models (LLMs)

The piece examines how hyperscalers and AI companies are using 144A private placement bonds to rapidly finance massive data centre and chip buildouts, bypassing slower public debt markets. It details deal structures involving firms like Broadcom, Meta, and SpaceX, with private credit providers such as Apollo, Blackstone, and KKR backstopping or syndicating the debt. The author warns of growing concentration risk, potential credit downgrades, and the limits of leverage as AI capital needs balloon into the trillions.
