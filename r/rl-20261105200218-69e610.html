Why We Can't Learn to Stop Worrying and Love This Economy

This podcast episode explores the disconnect between strong economic data and persistently negative American consumer sentiment. Personal finance reporter Oyin Adedoyin from The Wall Street Journal joins to share insights from her reporting on real people's financial lives, hopes, and stresses. Together they test theories to explain why Americans feel so bad about an economy that looks good on paper, even as consumer spending remains resilient.
