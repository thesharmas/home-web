Are Big Tech bonds crowding out the US Treasury?

The article investigates whether massive bond issuance by hyperscalers (Amazon, Alphabet, Meta, Oracle, SpaceX, Nvidia) — exceeding $200bn in 2026 — is crowding out US Treasury demand and pushing yields higher. While hyperscaler issuance does account for roughly an eighth of US Treasury duration supply, a broader look at the investment-grade corporate bond market shows no dramatic displacement of Treasuries. The author concludes that rising Treasury yields are better explained by fiscal deficits, economic strength, and geopolitics than by Big Tech bond issuance alone.
