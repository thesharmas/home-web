The Midterms Made Room for Housing in the AI Economy

Conor Sen argues that housing stocks in late 2026 are moving primarily on AI sentiment and political developments rather than housing-specific fundamentals, as midterm election results and new doubts about the AI buildout have caused AI-related stocks to fall while housing stocks rallied ~40%. He outlines three market scenarios for 2027—stagflationary crowding out, a Goldilocks slowdown in AI capex that relieves interest rate pressure, and an outright recession—each implying very different mortgage rate and housing market outcomes.
