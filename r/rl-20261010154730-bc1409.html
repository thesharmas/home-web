Is China building an air base in Laos?

Western governments and analysts are concerned that China may be establishing its first air base in mainland South-East Asia at Ban Keun, Laos, following a joint military facility announcement in late September 2026. Laos and China insist the site is merely a pilot training school, but the country's deep financial dependence on China—around $5bn in debt, roughly 30% of GDP—limits its ability to resist Chinese requests. America has little leverage to counter Chinese influence in Laos, given lingering resentment over Vietnam-era bombing, Trump-era tariffs, and deportation disputes.
