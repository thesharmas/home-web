The dark arts of securitisation

Satyajit Das argues that asset-backed securities and securitisation, while useful, carry poorly understood risks including embedded leverage, correlation risk, and credit rating migration risk. Using a worked example, he shows how equity tranche investors can suffer losses 33 times greater than pro-rata investors from the same default events. He warns that ABS "performs until it does not" and that investors will pay a steep price in the next financial crisis for underestimating these structural dangers.
