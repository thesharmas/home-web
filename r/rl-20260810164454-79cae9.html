Just how big is the hidden leverage of AI hyperscalers?

AI hyperscalers appear financially conservative by conventional debt metrics, but Goldman Sachs has identified $1.5 trillion in aggregate lease commitments—$1 trillion of which doesn't appear on balance sheets due to GAAP accounting rules for uncommenced leases. On top of this, Morgan Stanley counts a further $982 billion in purchase commitments for chips, computing power and electricity across major hyperscalers. The article questions whether the massive data centre capex will ultimately deliver sufficient returns to service these obligations.
