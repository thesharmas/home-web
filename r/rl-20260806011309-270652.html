MerchantBench: A 365-day seller-side environment

MerchantBench is a simulated 365-day seller-side environment that couples a time-varying supplier marketplace with a persistent merchant store and order-level customer outcomes. An agent acts every 12 hours across four linked decisions: sourcing, listing and pricing, cash-flow management, and feedback adaptation. The benchmark uses 98,843 real product records, 365 simulated days, and 26 merchant tools.
