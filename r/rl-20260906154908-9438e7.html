Is Stephen Miran right?

FT Alphaville examines whether Stephen Miran's argument that rising bond yields reflect higher expected long-run growth rather than fiscal or monetary credibility concerns has merit. The piece highlights Deutsche Bank's George Saravelos and Matt Klein's arguments that a global capex cycle in AI and strategic autonomy is driving remarkable economic resilience, with data surprises at record-long positive streaks. The 10y10y US Treasury forward rate returning to early-2000s levels is cited as evidence that the post-2008 'new normal' of low growth and low rates may finally be ending.
