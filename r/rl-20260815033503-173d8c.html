Nvidia Close to Deal to Guarantee About $100 Billion in Credit for OpenAI

Nvidia is close to providing roughly $100 billion in credit support to help OpenAI lease and finance the first phase of a massive AI data center campus in Ohio, which could ultimately cost $500 billion and require 10 gigawatts of power. SB Energy, majority-owned by SoftBank, is developing the facility on largely federally controlled land, with the project originating from a U.S.-Japan trade agreement.
