Death Becomes Him

Oliver Peyton, once famous for running trendy London restaurants and club nights in the 1990s, has reinvented himself as the founder of Exit Here, an upscale funeral parlour with branches in Chiswick, Crouch End, and Chelsea. Inspired by the impersonal nature of his father's 2010 funeral, Peyton aims to disrupt the staid British funeral industry by offering personalised, character-driven send-offs that can include music, dancing, and multi-day celebrations.
