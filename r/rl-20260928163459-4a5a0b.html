AI hyperscalers are transforming debt

AI hyperscalers—Amazon, Alphabet, Meta, Microsoft and Oracle—are raising an unprecedented volume of debt globally, with Goldman Sachs estimating ~$500bn in AI-linked financing so far this year and over $1tn more expected in coming years. Their borrowing is distorting credit markets worldwide, forcing other corporate and sovereign borrowers to time their debt issuances around the tech giants, pushing up yields, and creating complex interconnections across the financial system.
