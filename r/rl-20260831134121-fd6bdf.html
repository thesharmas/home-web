Happy birthday to the First Index Investment Trust

Fifty years after Vanguard's First Index Investment Trust launched in August 1976 with a dismal $11.32mn raise against a $150mn target, Robin Wigglesworth recounts the fund's troubled origins — rooted more in corporate revenge than investment philosophy — and its long slog to relevance. The piece traces how the fund, derided as "Bogle's Folly," didn't cross $100mn until 1981 and only surpassed Fidelity's Magellan as the world's largest fund in 2000, by which point it managed $1.7tn today. Wigglesworth also highlights how the S&P 500's concentration has grown dramatically since FIIT's launch…
