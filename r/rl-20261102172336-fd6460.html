SpaceX's maiden results as a public company dramatically revised AI infrastructure forecasts upward, with Elon Musk guiding to 2.5–3GW of compute by end-2026 and a possible 10–20GW in 2027. This single company accounted for 100% of current-year hyperscaler capex upgrades and 62% of 2027–2028 upgrades over the past quarter. The piece questions how much weight analysts should place on Musk's characteristically bullish and historically unreliable guidance.
