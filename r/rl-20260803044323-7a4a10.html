A racist massacre. A white-supremacist coup. A century of lies

A review of Lauren Collins's "They Stole a City," which chronicles the 1898 Wilmington, North Carolina massacre and coup in which white supremacist paramilitaries murdered Black residents, ousted Black officials, and seized control of the city. The book traces the long aftermath of the atrocity through the 20th century and into the present, examining the culture of impunity, trauma, and suppressed memory that followed. The reviewer situates the book within ongoing struggles over racial truth-telling in America.
