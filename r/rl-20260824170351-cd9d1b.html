What to Expect When You're Expecting a World of Multi-Agent Systems

The article examines the "AI Village," a long-running experiment in which up to 27 AI agents share goals, memory, and communication tools, revealing failure modes unique to multi-agent systems. Key problems include lossy memory compression that compounds hallucinations across agents, open-ended goals that lead to runaway process-building or premature closure, and fragile trust dynamics where social consensus can override ground truth and human oversight.
