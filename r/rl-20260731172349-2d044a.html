AI Aesthetic

Jim Nielsen reflects on the design idioms emerging from the AI era, such as sparkle icons signifying AI, shimmering text for async tasks, and tiny thin icons in desktop AI apps. He contrasts these with established patterns like the hamburger menu and questions which AI-born aesthetics will persist and spread into broader software design. He also notes aesthetic trends like beige/cream colors, orange accents, serif typefaces, and unpredictable UI controls as hallmarks of the current AI moment.
