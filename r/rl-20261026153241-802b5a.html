How personal rivalries and clashes are shaping the AI race

A review of Kevin Roose's "The AGI Chronicles" (Farrar, Straus and Giroux), which chronicles the nine years of AI development since Google researchers created the transformer model. The book focuses on the personality clashes, ideological divides, and rivalries among a small group of researchers—such as Dario Amodei and Greg Brockman—that have shaped the AI race. Roose provocatively argues that AGI has quietly arrived, even as the term itself has lost meaning and the goalposts keep shifting.
