Noam Brown — Agent Swarms, Alignment, Recursive Self-Improvement

Dwarkesh Patel interviews Noam Brown in an 80-minute episode covering multi-agent AI systems, the Navier-Stokes problem, and what recent breakthroughs in mathematics reveal about recursive self-improvement.
