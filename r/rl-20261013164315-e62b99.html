How governments spend money

The article uses OECD COFOG data to compare government spending as a share of GDP across countries, highlighting that France spends 57% of GDP versus the US at 39%, with France and Italy leading on social protection and pensions. Surprisingly, the US government outspends European universal-healthcare nations on health and education combined. The piece notes that debt service and defence costs are likely to grow across most economies, amid IMF warnings to rebuild public finances.
