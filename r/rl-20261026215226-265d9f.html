Building Reasoning Models from the Ground Up: A Full Video Course

Sebastian Raschka presents an 11-hour, eight-video course on building reasoning models from scratch in Python and PyTorch, covering evaluation, inference-time scaling, reinforcement learning (GRPO/RLVR), and distillation. The course uses a pre-trained Qwen3 0.6B model as a starting point and accompanies his book 'Build a Reasoning Model (From Scratch).' It also contextualizes reasoning models within the broader LLM-agent landscape, explaining how they serve as the 'engine' inside agent harnesses.
