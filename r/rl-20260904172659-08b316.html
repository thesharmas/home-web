VC isn't VC anymore — understanding the rise of Cancer Capital

Anil Dash, a veteran startup CEO and founder, argues that venture capital has transformed into what he calls "Cancer Capital" — a handful of mega-firms that have grown so large and legally restructured themselves so thoroughly that they are no longer accountable to markets, regulators, or voters. He contends these firms, exemplified by Andreessen Horowitz, have shifted from funding innovation to wielding concentrated political and financial power, funneling hundreds of millions into politics and engaging in self-dealing that insulates them from risk.
