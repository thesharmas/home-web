Donald Trump's least favourite thing

The article examines how the Trump administration's hostility toward wind and solar energy is slowing renewable development in the US through a combination of ended tax credits, halted federal permits, paid buyouts of offshore wind projects, and bureaucratic bottlenecks at the renamed Department of War. Offshore wind has been hit hardest given its high costs and dependence on federal permitting, while onshore wind faces delays from stalled national-security reviews affecting over 150 projects.
