You Are No Longer Invited to Dinner

Derek Thompson examines the dramatic decline of social hosting in America, citing data showing that the share of Americans who host friends or family monthly has collapsed from 42% in 1975 to just 12% in 2026. He explores four key explanations: the time pressures of dual-earner households, the rise of intensive parenting, shrinking friendship networks especially among poorer and less-educated Americans, and the growing comfort and convenience of solitary home entertainment. Thompson frames this as a 'coordination failure' driven by technology that makes being alone easier and more enjoyable…
