Cerebras Shares Fall 16% On A Decline In Hardware Revenue

Cerebras, an AI chip designer, saw its shares fall 16% after its second earnings call despite reporting 74% revenue growth to $180 million for the quarter. Hardware sales declined significantly to $54 million, about half of the prior quarter, alarming investors. Wall Street reacted negatively amid concerns about competition and the hardware revenue decline.
