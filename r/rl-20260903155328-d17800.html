Why Do We Love Gold So Much?

This podcast episode explores why gold has such enduring appeal as a store of value, examining both its physical properties and its long history as a collective obsession. Jacob Goldstein, author of 'Money: The True Story of a Made-Up Thing,' explains the real reasons gold is seen as a stable alternative to currencies. The episode was prompted by gold's strong performance in August, driven by buying from individuals, investors, and central banks worldwide.
