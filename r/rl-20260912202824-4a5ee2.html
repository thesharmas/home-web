The Private Equity Confidence Game

This episode of Slate Money features author William D. Cohan discussing his book Money to Burn, which investigates private-equity firm Apollo Global Management and its billionaire co-founder Leon Black. The conversation covers Black's investment empire, the broader dominance of private equity on Wall Street, his personal scandals, and his controversial relationship with Jeffrey Epstein.
