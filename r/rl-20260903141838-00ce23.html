TikTok Videos: 4.5 billion posts with engagement metrics

A Hugging Face dataset containing 4.5 billion TikTok video records collected via TikTok's private Android API over roughly three weeks, stored as 27 zstd-compressed Parquet files totalling ~289 GB. Each row represents one unique video and includes fields such as caption, engagement counts (views, likes, comments, shares, saves), sound metadata, country, and language. The dataset is released for research use, with caveats around snapshot timing, non-shuffled row ordering, absence of author identity, and GDPR/CCPA obligations.
