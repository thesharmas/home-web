The corgi-inspired ETF provider that wants to disrupt BlackRock

Corgi Funds, a San Francisco-based ETF provider founded in 2024 and backed by $266mn in VC funding, has rapidly filed for 550 ETFs with the SEC, aiming to surpass BlackRocks 488 US ETFs. The company, which is an offshoot of an AI-native insurance startup, is pursuing a spaghetti cannon strategy of launching hundreds of thematic, leveraged, and broad-market funds at fees that significantly undercut incumbents. Industry observers are intrigued but skeptical, noting that the economics of low-fee ETFs remain unproven without substantial distribution and assets under management.
