Bessent gets Drucked

FT columnist Robin Wigglesworth covers Stanley Druckenmiller's WSJ op-ed criticising US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent's bond buyback programme, arguing it amounts to price management rather than liquidity management. Druckenmiller, a longtime mentor of Bessent, warns that suppressing long-term yields subsidises fiscal procrastination and erodes the Treasury market's hard-won credibility. Wigglesworth agrees, noting that the buybacks are too small to meaningfully affect net long-dated issuance and that escalating commitments are undermining Bessent's credibility with markets.
