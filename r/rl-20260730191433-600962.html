How the Iranian Economy Actually Works

This episode of Bloomberg's Odd Lots podcast features Yeganeh Torbati and Bozorgmehr Sharafedin discussing their book 'Stolen Revolution: Betrayal and Hope in Modern Iran,' which examines how Iran's economy transformed after the 1979 revolution. They describe Iran as a 'mafia state' where political factionalism and cronyism are embedded in the country's political economy. The conversation covers how average Iranians navigate this system, the treatment of Iran's tech sector, and why recent peace talks may not significantly alter the economy's structure.
