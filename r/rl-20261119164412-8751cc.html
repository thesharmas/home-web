Whey too much cheese

The US dairy industry has surged in cheese and whey production, driven by soaring global protein demand and an $8bn investment in whey-processing plants. While European and New Zealand competitors face herd reductions due to environmental regulations, US dairy cow numbers have hit a 30-year high, enabling hyper-competitive cheese exports. The article draws a parallel between America's emerging cheese dominance and China's role in electric vehicles and solar panels.
