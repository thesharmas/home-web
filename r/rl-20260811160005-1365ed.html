SpaceX's 'very achievable' $100bn revenue run rate target

SpaceX announced on its inaugural earnings call that it would reach a $100bn annualised revenue run rate by end of 2026, up from just over $30bn currently. Deutsche Bank analysts back the target as "very achievable," driven primarily by neocloud contracts (including deals with Anthropic, Google, and a suspected US government agreement) and the expected contribution from the Cursor acquisition. The bank also notes SpaceX's capex could reach $200bn next year as it targets deploying 5-10 GW of compute capacity, likely requiring over $100bn in additional debt financing.
