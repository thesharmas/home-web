Stablecoins and (Non)Crypto Shocks: A 2026 Update

The post documents the continued rapid growth of U.S. dollar stablecoins to ~$308 billion in market cap through mid-2026, coinciding with passage of the GENIUS Act. It then uses the 2023 Silicon Valley Bank failure as a case study to examine how non-crypto shocks affect stablecoin reserve composition, finding that USDC's Circle Reserve Fund sharply reduced interest-rate risk (shorter maturities) while dramatically increasing repo holdings and shifting bank deposits toward GSIBs.
