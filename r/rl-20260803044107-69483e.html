How Tom Holland became the world's highest-grossing young actor

The article examines why Tom Holland, starring in two major 2026 blockbusters, has become the highest-grossing actor aged 30 or under with nearly $12bn in film revenue. It outlines four lessons from his success: start young, join a blockbuster franchise early, prioritize reliability over prestige, and—tongue-in-cheek—marry well, noting his marriage to co-star Zendaya.
