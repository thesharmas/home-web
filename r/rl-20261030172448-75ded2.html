Palantir shareholders want to know when they'll get their money back

Retail investors dominate Palantir's shareholder base and are increasingly vocal about wanting capital returns — dividends, buybacks, or stock splits — ahead of Q3 2026 earnings. RBC Capital Markets analysis finds that 46% of upvoted questions on Palantir's public message board focus on cash returns, up 30 percentage points from the prior quarter, suggesting growing frustration with management's inaction despite a ~$9B cash balance. RBC, which has held an underperform rating on Palantir since May 2022, warns that this fixation on near-term returns may crowd out harder strategic questions…
