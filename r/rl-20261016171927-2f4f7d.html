Sizing CoreWeave's 'unlimited' insurance capital

FT Alphaville's Toby Nangle examines CoreWeave co-founder Brannin McBee's claim that insurance capital is effectively "unlimited," stress-testing it against FSB and Fed data. Insurance companies hold roughly $39tn globally—less than 8% of total financial assets—with US life and annuity insurers' general account corporate debt holdings amounting to around $3.4tn.
