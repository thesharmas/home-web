Nvidia Weighs Radical Idea: Less Rubin Ultra Chip Memory

Nvidia is testing multiple versions of its next-generation Rubin Ultra GPU with significantly less high-bandwidth memory than originally announced—as little as 192GB or 256GB compared to the planned 1TB—due to a shortage of advanced HBM4E chips. The memory reduction could affect performance but might also produce cheaper chip options suitable for many AI workloads, and Nvidia may offset the impact through server-level design improvements like faster networking.
