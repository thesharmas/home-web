Kansas City Fed President Jeffrey Schmid on the First (Jackson Hole Under Kevin Warsh)

Bloomberg's podcast returns to the Jackson Hole Fed symposium, the first under new Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh, to discuss the current monetary policy landscape including high bond yields, above-target inflation, and AI's uncertain economic impact. Kansas City Fed President Jeffrey Schmid shares his views on baby boomer retirements and their effect on the labor force, his recent FOMC votes, and the symposium's focus on payments. The episode offers a preview of what to expect from Warsh's debut speech and how it may signal a departure from the Powell era.
