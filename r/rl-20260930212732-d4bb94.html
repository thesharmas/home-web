The greatest advance in consulting since the quadrant

A satirical Bartleby column from The Economist, written as an excerpt from a mock business hagiography, recounts how a young consultant named Harbour Brandish supposedly revolutionized consulting by adding a fifth box to the classic 2x2 quadrant matrix, dubbing the new shape the 'quindrant.' The piece parodies the overwrought storytelling style of business books, complete with dramatic pauses, tearful salutes, and breathless comparisons to Fleming and the Wright brothers.
