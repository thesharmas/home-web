Brazil falls into MAGA's orbit

Brazil's 2026 general election saw the left-wing Workers' Party and Lula suffer a sweeping defeat, with Flávio Bolsonaro—son of former president Jair Bolsonaro—poised to win the October 25th run-off and establish a right-wing dynasty. The result reflects voter exhaustion with Lula's government, corruption scandals, and economic frustration, rather than enthusiasm for the Bolsonaros. A Flávio presidency would likely align Brazil with the MAGA movement, reshape the country's institutions, and carry significant implications for regional geopolitics and the economy.
