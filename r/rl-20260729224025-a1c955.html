Demand Heats Up For Spend Now, Lease Later AI Financing

AI data center developers are increasingly turning to pre-lease bridge financing and cost reimbursement agreements (CRAs) to fund early-stage work—land, power studies, equipment—before tenants sign leases. Hyperscalers and AI labs are backstopping these arrangements to reduce lender risk, with Goldman Sachs noting a sharp rise in client inquiries for this type of capital. The approach carries real risk, as illustrated by Fermi America, whose $150 million advance from a prospective tenant (later revealed to be Amazon) was withdrawn after lease negotiations failed.
