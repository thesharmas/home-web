This is a very interesting time for trashy credit (and all those who love it)

FT Alphaville's Robin Wigglesworth uses the struggles of London credit hedge fund Arini Capital Management — down over 21% for the year — as a jumping-off point to explore the risks of chasing returns via low-rated CCC junk bonds and leverage. He explains how a systematic tilt toward riskier credit is a well-known but rarely acknowledged driver of supposed alpha in the bond world. The piece also examines whether the unusually sharp selloff in CCC-rated bonds signals broader economic trouble or is merely a cluster of idiosyncratic defaults in sectors like telecoms, cable, and media.
