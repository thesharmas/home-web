What's really going on with AI token price deflation?

The article examines the Silicon Data Token Expenditure Index, which shows a 52% drop in blended token prices, and argues the index conflates three distinct forces: technical efficiency gains, enterprises shifting to cheaper open-weight models, and competitive price wars. Analyst Jay Goldberg's data reveals a two-tier market where frontier (mostly American) LLM providers enjoy ~70% gross margins while open-weight (mostly Chinese) shops operate at ~20%, with cost of production falling faster than market prices.
