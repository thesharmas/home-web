What Should Not Be for Sale

This podcast episode explores the moral limits of markets, featuring philosopher Debra Satz and her book 'Why Some Things Should Not Be For Sale.' It contrasts her views with those of Nobel Prize-winning economist Al Roth, who argues markets can be beneficial even in controversial areas like organ trade. The episode questions which things should be kept off the market and why.
