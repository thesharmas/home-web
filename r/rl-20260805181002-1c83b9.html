A global workspace in language models

Anthropic researchers present evidence that Claude has developed an internal neural structure called the "J-space"—a small collection of representations that function analogously to the "global workspace" theorized in neuroscience as the basis for conscious access in humans. The J-space holds concepts Claude can report on, deliberately manipulate, and use for multi-step reasoning, while the vast majority of its processing runs automatically outside this space.
