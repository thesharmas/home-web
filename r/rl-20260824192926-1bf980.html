Jasmine Sun on What the AI Industry Got Wrong About Data Centers

Independent writer and Substacker Jasmine Sun joins Bloomberg's Odd Lots podcast to discuss the growing public backlash against data center construction in communities across the US. She draws on on-the-ground reporting from Wisconsin and Michigan to explain why opposition has surged despite data centers historically being welcomed and even incentivized by local governments. The episode explores what this 180-degree shift represents and what developers will need to do to gain community acceptance going forward.
