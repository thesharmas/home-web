Big Tobacco's lessons for Big Oil

The US Supreme Court is considering whether oil companies like Exxon Mobil and Suncor Energy can be held liable under state law for climate-related damages, in a case brought by Boulder, Colorado. The suit draws parallels to the 1990s tobacco litigation, alleging that oil companies concealed knowledge of fossil fuels' harms for decades. Key legal obstacles include whether federal law preempts state claims over interstate pollution, and the difficulty of establishing a direct causal link between corporate deception and climate inaction.
