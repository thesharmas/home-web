Eight of the best new novels to read now

The Economist recommends eight standout novels published between July and September 2026, spanning genres from crime fiction to speculative fiction to historical narrative. The list includes new works by authors such as Colson Whitehead, Marlon James, Emily St John Mandel, and Emma Cline, among others. Each brief review highlights themes ranging from art and identity to trauma, surveillance, and end-of-life choices.
