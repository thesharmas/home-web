What would Buffett think of Micron?

The piece uses curated quotes from Warren Buffett, Peter Lynch, Aswath Damodaran, Peter Thiel, Elon Musk, and others to examine whether Micron and the memory-chip industry should be treated as a commodity business and how that affects valuation. Key themes include the cyclicality of DRAM pricing, the persistent supply-demand imbalance in HBM memory driven by AI, and the challenge China's CXMT poses to the market. The author frames the analysis around how a value investor would assess earnings and long-term profitability through the cycle.
