Is Jane Street a hedge fund or a PTF?

Rupak Ghose argues that Jane Street is best described as a proprietary trading firm (PTF) with increasingly hedge-fund-like characteristics, including longer holding periods, large directional bets, and a massive ~$500bn balance sheet dwarfing peers like Citadel Securities and HRT. He uses 'hedge fund' as shorthand for this directional shift rather than a technical regulatory label. The piece also highlights Jane Street's extraordinary return on equity and its growing capital deployment as key differentiators from classical high-velocity PTF models.
