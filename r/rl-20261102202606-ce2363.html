The $300 Billion Structured Notes Market Is Booming

The Odd Lots podcast explores the booming US structured products market, which is on track to hit $300 billion in sales. Structured products are a fusion of a bank IOU with a derivative, and have become a popular vehicle for investors betting on the AI trade. Alex Micalizzi of Nomura explains how these products are created, how they work, and why sales have surged recently.
