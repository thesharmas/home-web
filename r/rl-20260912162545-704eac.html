Woke 1 was about identity. Woke 2 is about class

The Economist's Lexington column argues that the American left is undergoing a shift from Woke 1—focused on race and gender identity—to Woke 2, which emphasizes class and economic populism. Using the New York Post's mockery of DSA members' privileged backgrounds as a jumping-off point, the piece traces how electoral defeats, Trump's inroads with non-white working-class voters, and Zohran Mamdani's inclusive affordability message have pushed Democrats toward economic rather than identity-based politics.
