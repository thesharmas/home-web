The dark underbelly of "Paw Patrol"

The Economist examines the cultural debate surrounding the hit children's cartoon "Paw Patrol," exploring accusations that it is both left-wing and authoritarian neoliberal propaganda. The piece acknowledges that while such critiques may seem like culture-war excess, stories told to children do carry values worth scrutinizing. It ultimately suggests psychoanalysis—rather than ideology—as the better lens, arguing the show fulfils children's wish to solve the problems of anxious, overwhelmed adults.
