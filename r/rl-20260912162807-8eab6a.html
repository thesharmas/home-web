Chandrababu Naidu's techno-utopian vision for his Indian state

Chandrababu Naidu, the 76-year-old chief minister of Andhra Pradesh, is pursuing an ambitious plan to build Amaravati, a massive greenfield capital city, while positioning his state as a hub for advanced manufacturing, data centres, and quantum computing. His efforts have attracted major investments including a 1 GW Google AI data-centre project, but critics warn that Amaravati is an over-ambitious vanity project whose financing depends on speculative land-price increases and fragile political alliances.
