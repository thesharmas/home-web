Building bad

The piece examines the mass acquisition and physical destruction of books by AI companies for training data, citing Anthropic's 'Project Panama' and similar efforts by other labs, framing it as part of a broader pattern of 'radical indifference' to culture and meaning. The author contrasts these companies' aggressive IP extraction with their vocal complaints about competitors doing the same to their own models, arguing this reflects deep bad faith.
