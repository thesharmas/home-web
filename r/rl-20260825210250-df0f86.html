The Cult of Raising Cane's

Raising Cane's has grown into America's third-biggest chicken chain by doing exactly one thing—serving chicken fingers with a single dipping sauce—while inspiring near-religious fan devotion called Caniacs who camp overnight for new openings. The chain's privately held status, founder-led culture, minimalist menu, and savvy viral marketing with celebrities and social media have fueled a doubling of domestic sales to nearly $5 billion in five years.
