Slopfiling

FT Alphaville's Louis Ashworth documents a phenomenon dubbed 'slopfiling': companies inadvertently revealing their use of ChatGPT in SEC and other regulatory filings by leaving 'utm_source=chatgpt.com' tracking slugs in their citations. The offenders tend to be smaller, non-household-name firms using AI to populate boilerplate narrative sections such as market-size estimates, sometimes with hallucinated or mismatched figures. The author concludes that while mildly amusing, filing boilerplate was low-quality before LLMs and investors shouldn't pay it much heed.
