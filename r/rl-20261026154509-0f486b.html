Squeeze into those skinny jeans: millennial culture is back

Millennial culture is experiencing a major revival as the generation approaches peak spending power and takes on creative decision-making roles in entertainment and media. Hollywood, the music industry, gaming, and fashion are all mining late-1990s and 2000s nostalgia through reboots, reunion tours, and retro product launches. Technology—particularly streaming platforms and social media—is amplifying and accelerating this nostalgia cycle, potentially making the millennial revival the most powerful yet, though also potentially the most short-lived.
