This Isn't How Credit Cycles End

Itai Lourie argues that last week's sharp selloff in semiconductors and AI-related equities does not signal the end of the AI bubble or a credit cycle bust. He points to robust investment-grade bond markets—with megacap deals heavily oversubscribed and pricing tighter than talk even as chip stocks fell 13%—as evidence that credit channels remain open. True credit cycle endings, he contends, only come when the best names can no longer issue debt, which is far from the current reality.
