Wild Beast and Fickle, Too: Market is Misreading Hyperscaler Spreads

The piece argues that July 2026 market panic over hyperscaler credit spread widening is being misread: the spread increases reflect a surge in AI-linked bond supply ($165B in H1) rather than credit stress or default risk. The author contends that long credit for major hyperscalers (excluding SpaceX and Oracle) is a reasonable buy once supply clears, while urging readers to hedge duration and take primary risk in equities. A key warning is to watch hyperscaler OAS versus junk as the real signal—if they converge, the supply narrative breaks down.
