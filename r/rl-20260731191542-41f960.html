The Real Reason that Substack is Collapsing

Scott Carney argues that Substack's growth strategy—adding Twitter-like Notes, video, and free content—has accelerated subscription fatigue, eroding paid subscriber revenue for independent writers even as overall platform engagement rose. He contends the platform failed to address a structural problem: a finite pool of readers willing to pay for newsletters competing against each other and against Netflix, HBO, and other subscription services. Carney reflects on his own declining subscriber numbers and his pivot toward YouTube sponsorships as a more sustainable income source.
