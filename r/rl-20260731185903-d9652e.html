The hidden financial costs of chasing AI supremacy and who is unwillingly footing the bill

The article argues that Meta and Microsoft are materially over-reporting profitability by using share buybacks to fulfill RSU obligations to AI talent hires, keeping a significant recurring compensation cost off the income statement. Using Meta's financial notes, the author estimates this hidden cost has eroded roughly 10 percentage points of EBIT margin annually for Meta and 2 points for Microsoft, with Meta's true free cash flow nearly wiped out in FY25.
