How Donald Trump remade America's government

A review of Michael Lewis's book "Blockers," which chronicles federal workers who resisted the Trump administration's DOGE-led assault on the federal bureaucracy. Lewis argues that while government waste is a real problem, DOGE's slash-and-burn approach—driven by ideologically motivated young techies who misunderstood the machinery of government—made the state smaller in neither size nor cost, but dumber, crueller, and less accountable. The review highlights specific cases of DOGE's disregard for law, including illegal access to IRS taxpayer data used to target undocumented immigrants.
