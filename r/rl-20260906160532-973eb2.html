Reinforcement Learning for LLMs: The Complete Guide

This comprehensive guide traces the evolution of reinforcement learning (RL) for large language models from first principles, covering the formal RL framework, policy gradient algorithms (VPG, REINFORCE, RLOO, PPO), and key concepts like KL divergence and importance sampling. It explains how RL maps onto LLM training through MDP and bandit formulations, and distinguishes between RLHF (human preference rewards) and RLVR (verifiable rewards). The piece serves as a standalone reference synthesizing years of research, with implementations in PyTorch and links to deeper dives on each topic.
