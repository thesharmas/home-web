Who is Anthropic's auditor — and why should we care?

FT Alphaville investigates which Big Four accounting firm audits Anthropic ahead of its anticipated IPO, concluding it is likely Ernst & Young by process of elimination: Deloitte audits rival OpenAI, while PwC, KPMG, and Deloitte all have client-facing partnership or alliance agreements with Anthropic that would violate auditor independence rules. The piece explains why this matters — the first of Anthropic or OpenAI to IPO will expose previously opaque accounting for the deeply intertwined AI ecosystem of hyperscalers, investments, and alliances.
