The US Treasury is buying long bonds, but not very many

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced plans to at least double long-end liquidity support buyback operations to prop up the US bond market, but the actual net impact is modest. Despite the announcement, the Treasury still plans to issue a net ~$97bn of new 20-30 year bonds this quarter, down only ~$6bn from the prior $103bn plan. Bond markets were unimpressed, with long-dated yields continuing to rise and the intervention failing to soothe investor concerns.
