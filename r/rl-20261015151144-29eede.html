Do dentists actually know what they're doing?

A first-person narrative spanning ages 17 to 33 in which the author visits a succession of dentists across multiple states, each offering contradictory diagnoses and treatments for the same jaw and dental problems. The piece traces a darkly comic pattern: every dentist confidently overrides the last, recommends something new ranging from tooth reshaping to $150,000 jaw surgery, and invokes a personal relationship ('if you were my son/grandson/kid') to close the sale.
