What Google's AI Shake-Up Means for Pichai Succession

Google elevated DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis to chair of its AI unit and chief scientist of Alphabet, while several DeepMind leaders including Jeff Dean departed to start their own firm. The piece argues the shake-up strengthens Google Cloud chief Thomas Kurian's chances as a Sundar Pichai successor, as Hassabis appears more interested in a scientific role than operational leadership. Additional news covers Figma's sharp revenue growth slowdown and stock drop, Shopify's strong earnings beat, and Uber's $10 billion planned investment in autonomous vehicles.
