Chattering central bankers are good, actually

Robin Wigglesworth examines Goldman Sachs research finding that increased central bank transparency and communication has lowered interest rate volatility by around 10% and improved monetary policy transmission. The research also pushes back on common criticisms — that more chatter increases volatility or that forward guidance makes central banks slower to react to new data. The piece uses these findings to assess the potential costs of the Fed's new communications task force scaling back FOMC transparency.
