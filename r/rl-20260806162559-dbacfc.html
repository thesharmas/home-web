Getting Back in the Loop

Ian Kar returns to publishing after a difficult year marked by his father's heart attack and an extended stay at his parents' home, reflecting on family, caregiving, and the asymmetry of parent-child relationships. He argues that boredom and distance from city life rekindled his motivation to write and publish online, framing the internet as a homing beacon for like-minded people. He previews upcoming pieces on the U.S.-China AI adoption race, AI-generated slop, and the drift toward neofeudalism in the modern economy.
