What's Behind the Big Surge in US Government Bond Yields?

Global bond yields have hit their highest levels since 2008, with the 30-year US Treasury touching 5%, amid a surprise expansion of the Treasury's bond buyback program and a hawkish speech by Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh at Jackson Hole. Bloomberg's Odd Lots podcast speaks with Stanford Professor Darrell Duffie, a bond market expert who presented at Jackson Hole in 2023, to explore what is driving yields higher. The conversation covers policy options for bringing yields down and the challenge of shrinking the Fed's balance sheet.
