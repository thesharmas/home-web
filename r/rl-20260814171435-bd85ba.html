Temperature Zero for Culture: Why Everything Is Starting to Look the Same

Lauren Leek argues that recommendation algorithms, by minimising prediction error, systematically suppress variance and drive cultural, physical, and synthetic convergence toward the mean—producing more sameness the more personalised they become. Drawing on data from London high streets, Billboard lyrics, film ratings, and synthetic persona experiments, she shows the mechanism is the classic exploitation-versus-exploration trade-off.
