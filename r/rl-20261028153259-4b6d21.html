Coreweave Debt - A Not So Basic Overview

The piece provides a detailed breakdown of CoreWeave's debt stack, including SPV-level secured DDTLs, parent-level unsecured senior notes, and convertible notes, arguing that consensus and sell-side projections significantly underestimate the company's leverage trajectory. The author highlights that CDS markets imply a ~63% five-year cumulative default probability and that free cash flow is projected to remain deeply negative through at least 2028. The piece concludes with a warning to avoid neoclouds and a provocative prediction of an eventual government-sponsored AI bailout.
