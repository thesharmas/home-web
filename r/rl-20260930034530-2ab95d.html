Tanker rates are on the crest of a wave

A Gulf war has caused tanker charter rates to reach historic highs, with average daily spot rates for very large crude carriers (VLCCs) exceeding $650,000—over six times January levels. Inefficiencies around the Strait of Hormuz, Houthi threats in the Red Sea, and rerouting through Egypt have stretched fleet capacity and pushed rates for smaller tankers up as well. Tanker owners are reaping a bonanza while refineries, buffered by record margins, absorb transport costs that can exceed $25 a barrel.
