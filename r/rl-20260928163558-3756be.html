OK, so where are all these data centres?

Jefferies and Morgan Stanley research, using satellite imagery from SynMax Intelligence, finds a significant gap between GPU chip sales forecasts and actual US data centre capacity that can be energised. Between 16–18 GW of gross capacity may come online in 2026, well short of what chip sales imply, with land clearing having plateaued as a warning sign for outer years. Beyond power, chip packaging capacity at overseas facilities is identified as the most immediate bottleneck translating semiconductor demand into operational data centres.
