Rohit Sharma — A Founder's Guide to Debt Capital

Rohit Sharma, founder of Kanmon (embedded lending infrastructure for vertical SaaS), announces a free guide called 'A Founder's Guide to Debt Capital' as a follow-up to his DebtStack tool. The guide covers the full lifecycle of debt facilities for founders, including warehouse vs. forward flow structures, term sheet mechanics, true cost of capital, and life with a lender. It includes practical checklists, a glossary, and integration with a cost simulator.
