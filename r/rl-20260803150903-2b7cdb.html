The Tungsten Market Is Warning of an Upcoming War

Bloomberg Opinion columnist David Fickling discusses tungsten mining as a geopolitical indicator, describing it as 'a century old prediction market for war.' He explores tungsten's strategic importance in war manufacturing due to its high density and melting point, and visits Australia's Dolphin Mine, which historically opens during wartime and closes during peace. Fickling examines why investors are again interested in tungsten and why sustaining production outside China has proven difficult.
