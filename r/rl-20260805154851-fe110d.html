What happens to yields in between FOMC meetings?

The piece examines how Treasury yield price discovery has shifted from occurring almost exclusively around FOMC meeting windows (pre-2021) to happening predominantly between meetings since the Fed began QT in 2022. Drawing on research by Sebastian Hillenbrand, the author shows that 87% of the 32-year secular decline in 10-year yields through 2021 was concentrated in three-day FOMC windows, while non-FOMC days contributed almost nothing.
