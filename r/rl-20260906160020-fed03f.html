The Story I Almost Didn't Publish: Abliteration AI

AJ Green describes his hesitation to publish about a newly released "abliterated" AI model — an open-weight model with its safety refusals deliberately removed — because of its potential for misuse. He argues that as open-weight models rapidly close the gap with closed frontier models, removing guardrails becomes an increasingly serious cybersecurity threat. Ultimately he concludes that awareness (without enablement) is essential, and that defensive AI may be the only scalable answer to offensive AI.
