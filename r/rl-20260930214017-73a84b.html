You can't trade your way through the AI apocalypse

The article explores whether investors can financially hedge against the existential and catastrophic risks posed by advanced AI systems. While some milder AI risks are already being priced into markets—such as cybersecurity stocks rising on hacking fears—truly extreme scenarios like an AI takeover are inherently unhedgeable, since any counterparty to such insurance would not survive to pay out. The piece argues that AI danger, unlike conventional financial risks, resists quantification and market discipline.
