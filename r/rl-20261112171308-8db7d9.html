The AI megatrade's mounting portfolio concentration risk problem

As AI hyperscalers plan to issue trillions in debt to fund infrastructure buildout, fixed income portfolio managers are running into concentration limits that typically cap individual issuer exposure at 1-2% of a portfolio. This structural constraint means credit markets may struggle to absorb the scale of AI-related debt issuance, with sophisticated investors seeing through off-balance-sheet structures to underlying tenant risk. The result is a widening of credit spreads on hyperscaler and AI chip company debt that reflects not deteriorating creditworthiness but simple portfolio indigestion.
