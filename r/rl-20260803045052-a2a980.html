America has become an entrepreneur's paradise

America is experiencing a sustained entrepreneurship boom, with new-business applications running at nearly double pre-pandemic levels as of mid-2026. Three factors are driving this: a tight labor market pushing job-seekers toward self-employment, shifting consumption patterns (ageing population, niche e-commerce), and AI tools that dramatically lower the cost and complexity of starting a business. The rise of the "solopreneur" is notable, with AI filling capability gaps that once required hiring staff, though some entrepreneurs do go on to build larger teams.
