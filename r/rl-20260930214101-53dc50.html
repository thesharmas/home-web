Turkey's investment-fund fiasco

A wave of investment fund collapses in Turkey has exposed alleged Ponzi-like schemes, wiping $30bn from the stock market in two days and leaving at least 450,000 investors facing losses. Regulators were slow to act despite early warnings, and the scandal has damaged Turkey's ambitions as a global financial hub and raised the prospect of an MSCI downgrade from emerging to frontier market status. The crisis reflects broader vulnerabilities, including sky-high inflation driving inexperienced retail investors into volatile equities.
