Brad Setser on the US's Unusual Japanese Yen Intervention

The US and Japan jointly intervened to halt the yen's slide, marking the first such collaboration in 15 years. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent took the unusual step of selling euros rather than dollars and used a little-known Federal Reserve repo facility. Brad Setser of the Council on Foreign Relations discusses why the Bank of Japan delayed rate hikes, broader East Asian currency weakness, and Japan's fiscal outlook.
