Nick Bostrom on What Happens If AI Solves All of Our Problems

Philosopher Nick Bostrom, known for his work on AI existential risk in 'Superintelligence', discusses his follow-up book 'Deep Utopia', which explores what human life and meaning might look like if AI solves all of humanity's problems. He examines two divergent futures for AI — catastrophic misalignment versus a fully 'solved world' — and considers what role humans might play when AI and robots can do everything better. The episode is part of Bloomberg's Odd Lots podcast.
