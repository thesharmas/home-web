The Michigan Senate primary is a fight for control of the Democrats

The August 4th Michigan Democratic Senate primary pits progressive Abdul El-Sayed against moderate Haley Stevens, with each trying to unite college-educated activists and centrists. The race is heavily influenced by AIPAC spending (~$30m) backing Stevens and anger over Israel policy fueling El-Sayed's insurgent campaign. Observers worry the intraparty battle mirrors the Republican factional warfare that preceded Trump's rise.
