America's largest home lender falls out of the hamster wheel

Todd H Baker argues that non-bank mortgage origination is a fundamentally broken business, using UWM Holdings' $452mn quarterly loss, suspended dividend, and $2bn rescue capital raise as the latest illustration. He outlines structural flaws including acute sensitivity to interest rates, negative convexity, and dependence on non-cash accounting, which scale and digitisation have failed to fix. He is skeptical that AI will resolve these deep structural problems, though UWM's rescue investors could profit if the Fed turns dovish.
