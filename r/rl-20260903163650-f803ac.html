San Francisco's Hot List Proves That The Ragebait Economy Is Still Raging

Kylie Robison examines the viral "San Francisco's Hot List," a curated ranking of the city's most dateable people launched by Celeste Amadon, which quickly drew massive online mockery of San Francisco's tech culture and aesthetics. The list featured a mix of high-profile tech personalities and lesser-known young professionals, some of whom were unaware they had been nominated. The piece situates the stunt within the broader "ragebait economy," noting how it provoked predictable ridicule while still commanding widespread engagement.
