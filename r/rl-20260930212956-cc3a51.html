Agent of whom?

Meta's new personal AI agent, Muse, is reshaping commerce and daily life by acting autonomously on behalf of consumers—booking tickets, filing claims, cancelling subscriptions, and switching services. The piece explores how widespread AI agents will strain governments (through exploding rights claims and court filings), disrupt companies that profit from consumer ignorance and inertia, and blur legal responsibility when agents cause harm. It poses fundamental questions about the relationship between users and their AI representatives, and who bears blame when autonomous agents act carelessly…
