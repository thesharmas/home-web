Emily Oster Doesn't Mind the Haters

This podcast episode features Emily Oster, an economics professor at Brown University, discussing how she applies economic analysis to health research. She addresses her controversial stances—such as her book challenging conventional pregnancy wisdom and her recommendation to reopen schools during the pandemic—and explores how gaps in scientific knowledge get filled by theories, political agendas, and misinformation. The conversation covers why health and science become politicized, how individuals can learn to parse data, and how to live with uncertainty.
