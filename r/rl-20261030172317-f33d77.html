If you think Triple-C bonds are having a rough time . . .

Triple-C rated bonds are experiencing significant distress, with US yields hitting 18% and European yields exceeding 21%. European Triple-C loans are faring even worse, with yields nearing 36%, and distressed loans (trading below 80 cents) are appearing broadly across loan markets. The author argues that the Triple-C loan market may serve as a useful proxy for stress in private credit, given that many loans refinanced into private credit in 2023-24 were rated just one notch above CCC.
