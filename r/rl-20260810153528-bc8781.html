Notes on Midtraining

This article provides a comprehensive overview of continued pretraining (CPT) and midtraining as intermediate stages in LLM training pipelines, analyzing best practices from numerous industry tech reports and research papers. Key themes include using short proxy experiments to vet data sources and tune hyperparameters, the importance of gradually annealing data mixtures toward higher-quality and domain-specific data rather than abrupt switches, and optimizing midtraining not just for immediate benchmark performance but for downstream post-trainability.
