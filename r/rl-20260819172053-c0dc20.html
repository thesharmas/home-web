The US economy is running hot

Ryan Avent argues that recent softer US economic data does not signal cyclical weakness but rather structural supply-side constraints: a shrinking labour force due to demographics and deportations, and household consumption being crowded out by government borrowing and massive AI-related investment via higher interest rates. Real yields are up significantly since the start of the war, while inflation expectations remain largely stable and financial conditions have actually loosened, suggesting the Fed has not yet brought demand into balance with supply.
