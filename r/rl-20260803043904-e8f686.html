The tide turns for a Florida darling and California loser

San Francisco is staging a comeback after pandemic-era decline, driven by a political shift toward pragmatism and an AI-fueled office market recovery, while Miami is losing momentum as surging housing costs and reduced immigration squeeze out lower-income residents. Both cities now risk becoming plutocrat enclaves, with affordable housing a central challenge for their new mayors. The reversal illustrates how pandemic-era "winners" and "losers" among American cities can swap fortunes within just a few years.
