Who is driving the massive surge in repo borrowing?

The FT's Alphaville column examines who is driving the surge in global repo market borrowing, now estimated at around $22tn. Hedge funds have emerged as the dominant borrowers, with their US repo borrowing nearly trebling since October 2022 to $3tn by January 2026, accounting for most of the market's recent growth. The article notes this concentration of short-term leverage has prompted the Federal Reserve to expand its balance sheet again, and questions whether Fed chair Kevin Warsh will attempt any structural intervention.
