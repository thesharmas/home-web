The terrifying threat of a genetically engineered plague

A review of Annie Jacobsen's "Biological War: A Scenario," which imagines a genetically modified pneumonic plague leaking from a Siberian lab and spreading globally, exposing catastrophic gaps in America's biodefences. The book details how detection failures, inadequate isolation capacity, and bureaucratic dysfunction allow the pathogen to spiral into societal collapse. The reviewer notes the book's timeliness but flags a surprising omission: the growing role of AI in lowering barriers to bioweapon development.
