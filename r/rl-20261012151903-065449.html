Death-spiral finance: altcoin edition

The piece examines CHAD, a perpetual preferred stock issued by DeFi Development Corp on Nasdaq, which mimics Strategy's bitcoin-treasury model but for Solana (SOL). The author explains how the death-spiral risk inherent in using perpetual preferred stock to buy volatile digital assets is amplified by Solana's historic volatility, and notes that CHAD's prospectus gives management near-total discretion over how proceeds are spent. The piece raises questions about Nasdaq's vetting standards for allowing such instruments to trade on its Capital Market tier.
