On the ownership of assets

Chris Dillow uses Gianni Infantino's failed attempt to sell a stake in the World Cup as a springboard to explore the economics of asset ownership, focusing on the hold-up problem and residual claimant theory. He traces how incomplete contracts push toward vertical integration and ownership by indispensable agents, drawing on Coase, Hart & Moore, and Marx. The piece ultimately argues that because externalities make society a de facto residual claimant on banks and polluters, there is an economic case for broader public or collective ownership of productive assets.
