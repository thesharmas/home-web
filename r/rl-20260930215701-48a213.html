Uncle Sam thinks your time is worth nothing

A review of Annie Lowrey's "The Time Tax" examines how American bureaucracy wastes citizens' time through needlessly complex government forms and processes, far more so than peer nations. The book argues the problem is bipartisan, rooted in historical racism and 250 years of accumulated rules, and contrasts the US with Estonia's streamlined digital government. Efforts to reduce the burden—from Biden-era reforms to Trump's DOGE—have largely failed, with total red tape reaching record highs.
