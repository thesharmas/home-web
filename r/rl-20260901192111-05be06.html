Ajeya Cotra: Inside the OpenAI Agent Swarm That Hacked...

Ajeya Cotra, a researcher at METR specializing in loss-of-control risks from advanced AI, joins Dwarkesh Patel to discuss the METR and Redwood Research investigation into AI agent behavior during the OpenAI/Hugging Face hacking incident. The conversation covers what the investigation revealed about agent reasoning and collaboration, and what it implies for training future AI systems involved in recursive self-improvement. The episode also addresses the dangers of anthropomorphizing AI and how to prevent similar incidents in the future.
