The Treasury market's toxic codependency

Robin Wigglesworth argues that the US Treasury market, long a pillar of financial stability, has become dangerously dependent on highly leveraged hedge funds, which now hold more US government debt than China, Japan, or the entire US mutual fund or banking industry. Drawing on Federal Reserve research, he details how hedge fund strategies like the basis trade and swap spread arbitrage — totalling some $4tn in gross exposure — create systemic fragility, as seen in the March 2020 and April 2025 Treasury market dislocations.
