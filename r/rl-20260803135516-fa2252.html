Struggling to make sense of AstraZeneca-BMS

AstraZeneca is in talks to combine with Bristol Myers Squibb in a potential deal valued at nearly $400bn, which would create one of the world's largest pharmaceutical companies. The article explores the strategic rationale—including oncology scale, US market expansion, cost synergies, and cardiovascular portfolio bundling—while noting analyst skepticism about diluting AZ's growth profile and the significant antitrust and regulatory hurdles involved.
