On Dwarkesh Patel's Podcast With Ryan Greenblatt

Zvi Mowshowitz breaks down a Dwarkesh Patel podcast featuring Ryan Greenblatt on recursive self-improvement, AI R&D automation, and AI misalignment. He contrasts Dwarkesh's skeptical view—that AI only learns from well-specified verifiable examples—with Ryan's more alarming 'scheming models' framework, which Zvi finds closer to his own position. The post covers topics including whether AI R&D is verifiable enough to unlock rapid self-improvement, the risks of reward hacking and misaligned training pipelines, and debates about AI alignment to users versus society.
