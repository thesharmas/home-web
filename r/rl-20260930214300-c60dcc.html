A contentious battle in Alaska may determine control of the Senate

The 2026 Alaska Senate race between Republican incumbent Dan Sullivan and Democrat Mary Peltola is rated a toss-up and could determine Senate control. Peltola, a native Alaskan and former congresswoman, benefits from Alaska's fluid political culture and voter anger over DOGE federal workforce cuts and high fuel costs, but faces scrutiny over a New York Times report alleging staff mistreatment. The Economist's model gives her a 56% chance of winning, contingent on her ability to attract Trump voters in a state the president carried by 13 points.
