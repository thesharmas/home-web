Moderna's cancer shot: exciting for investors, inconclusive for patients

Moderna's shares surged up to 113% after the company reported positive late-stage trial results for its personalised mRNA melanoma vaccine (INT), developed with Merck. While the interim results showed statistically significant improvements in recurrence-free survival, full data has not been released, making it difficult to assess the magnitude or durability of the benefit.
