The Government Report That Made Me Stop Trusting Our Statistical Agencies

Former Biden economic adviser Jared Bernstein argues that a new, unsigned Census Bureau report claiming 24,000 non-citizens voted in the 2020 election is deeply flawed and represents a dangerous politicization of U.S. statistical agencies. He explains that the report's record-linkage methodology is unreliable—lacking SSN-based matching—meaning the alleged noncitizen voters are more likely data errors than real cases, as corroborated by two former Census experts.
