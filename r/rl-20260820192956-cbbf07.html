AI and Alienation

This podcast episode examines how AI is concentrating wealth, disrupting labor markets, and straining infrastructure, framing it as the latest 'General Purpose Technology' humanity must adapt to. Cornell economist Rohit Lamba discusses AI's global spread, with a focus on India, where inequality, unreliable utilities, and youth unemployment make the stakes especially high. He offers frameworks for navigating the disruption with less pain than past technological transitions.
