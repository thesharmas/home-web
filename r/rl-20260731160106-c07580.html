Why Private Credit Got Entangled With Insurance

This Odd Lots episode explores how insurers have become a major driver of the private credit boom, with private equity firms striking deals with or acquiring insurance companies. Guests Andrew Granato and Pranjal Drall, authors of "Private Credit's State Backstop: How Private Equity Socializes Risk Through Insurers," explain the mutual benefits of the PE-insurance relationship and how it is reshaping the insurance industry. They also raise concerns about systemic risk, regulatory gaps, and the potential for taxpayers to ultimately bear the costs.
