Who bears the risk in Nvidia's $500 billion financing platform?

Sascha Steffen analyzes Nvidia's August 2026 announcement of $500 billion in third-party compute financing platforms organized with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR. He argues that the credit risk in these structures flows from short-lived GPU collateral through bankruptcy-remote SPVs into insurance general accounts, relabeled as investment-grade fixed income, and is only as sound as the offtaker — robust where hyperscalers hold firm take-or-pay contracts, fragile where AI labs are the ultimate payers.
