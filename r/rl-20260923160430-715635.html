Shall We Repeal the Laws of Economics – Part III

Howard Marks argues that the U.S. Treasury's attempts to suppress rising long-term interest rates through bond buybacks are cosmetic fixes that fail to address root causes: persistent inflation, chronic fiscal deficits, and surging capital demand from AI investment and federal debt refinancing. He contends that the only genuine solution is fiscal discipline—flattening deficit growth through higher revenues and spending restraint—rather than market interventions that distort fundamentals.
