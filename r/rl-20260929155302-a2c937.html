Fabulous Beautiful Debt

This episode of Slate Money features Felix Salmon, Elizabeth Spiers, and Emily Peck in conversation with Financial Times journalist Robin Wigglesworth, whose new book traces the history of bonds and how they shaped the modern world. The discussion covers Wigglesworth's research, the surprisingly compelling history of the bond market, and the current state of the Treasury market amid spiking global yields. A Slate Plus bonus segment addresses the rising cost of everyday goods, using a $60 loaf of bread as a focal point.
