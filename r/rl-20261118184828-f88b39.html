When's the crash? Ask a journalist

A new academic paper by researchers from London Business School and the University of Cambridge analyzes 170 years of financial media coverage using LLMs to build indices estimating bubble probability. The study finds journalists systematically over-warn about market crashes, yet when filtered to only the most extreme anxiety signals, the model accurately identifies major historical bubbles like the 2007 financial crisis and the 2000 dotcom peak.
