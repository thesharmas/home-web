What a $30bn Aussie data centre IPO debacle actually means

The article examines the collapse of the $5.5bn Australian IPO of Firmus Grid, an Nvidia-backed data centre business that sought a ~$30bn valuation, exploring why the deal fell apart when investors balked at the price after a late-stage Meta deal revised the numbers upward. Author Craig Coben, drawing on investment banking experience, explains the mechanics of failed IPOs — including mispriced ranges, client-bank trust breakdowns, and the cascade of order withdrawals once momentum falters.
