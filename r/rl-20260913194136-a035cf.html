The new age of the orator

The article examines how declining reading habits are giving rise to a new oral culture, where ideas spread through podcasts, TikToks, and public debate rather than text. Drawing on James Marriott's polemic "The New Dark Ages," it explores how oral communication favors concrete, emotionally resonant messaging over abstract reasoning, with political implications visible in figures like Trump and Kirk. It warns that while oral culture is not new, the retreat from deep reading risks losing the kind of complex, nuanced ideas that only written argument can sustain.
