What the Big Mac index reveals about a global currency beef

This Economist Leaders article marks 40 years of the Big Mac index, the publication's informal measure of purchasing power parity based on McDonald's burger prices. The piece examines what the index reveals about global currency misalignments and economic imbalances. It also explores how insights from 'burgernomics' can inform solutions to currency and economic problems worldwide.
