Do you really want a "slow life?"

In her 23rd month without a smartphone, the author reflects on what a slow, analog evening actually looks like day to day — weeding, cooking, reading, waiting. She argues that the real substance of a slow life is boredom and solitude, not the aesthetic performance of slowness, and that learning to sit with boredom rather than mask it is where genuine mental flourishing happens.
