What Andy Burnham's £80 T-shirt really tells us

Using Andy Burnham's expensive Manchester-made casual wear as a jumping-off point, Ed Conway explores the opacity of garment supply chains and how 'Made in X' labels reveal little about where a product truly originates. He traces the rise and fall of Manchester's textile industry to illustrate how decades of prioritising cheap global sourcing hollowed out domestic manufacturing capacity.
