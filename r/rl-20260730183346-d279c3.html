Trying to make sense of Warsh

The piece analyzes Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's press conference, which triggered a surge in US borrowing costs to a 19-year high despite no change in policy. The author explores Warsh's suggestion that rising market interest rates could substitute for a Fed rate hike, using Goldman Sachs's Financial Conditions Index framework to quantify the tightening effect of recent market moves (~33bps). The piece concludes with skepticism about whether deliberately injecting uncertainty premia into markets is a coherent or sustainable monetary policy strategy.
