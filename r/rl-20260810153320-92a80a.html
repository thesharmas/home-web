The Housing Recession is Over

Conor Sen argues that the U.S. housing recession that began in early 2022 is now over after roughly 4.5 years. While weakness persists in some markets and affordability remains challenging, inventory in Florida and Texas is falling, homebuilder margins are improving, unemployment is declining, and stock market wealth is supporting high-end demand. San Francisco stands out with sharp price and rent gains driven by the AI boom.
