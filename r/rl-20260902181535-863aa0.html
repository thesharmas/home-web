GoPro: a totally normal, unremarkable piece of merger activity

GoPro announced a merger with Starman Optical, a privately held optical-photonics company, with shareholders receiving $1.14 per share in cash plus ~10% equity in the combined entity. The deal follows a suspicious price surge tied to YouTuber Markiplier publicly disclosing an 8.5% stake and promoting GoPro's new camera, raising eyebrows about the timing. FT Alphaville notes that Starman itself has a thin corporate history, ongoing litigation, and the merger announcement was not yet accompanied by an SEC filing.
