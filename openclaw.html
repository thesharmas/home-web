Readlog

01

Remember everything you read

Send Readlog a screenshot, article link, podcast episode, YouTube video, or photo of a highlighted book page. It identifies the source, captures the important ideas, removes duplicates, and adds everything to one reading history. It also handles publications that require a login, including The Economist and The Information.

Impact: I can save something from almost anywhere without thinking about formats, apps, or filing systems — and find it again later.

Home Automation

02

A house that monitors itself and explains what is happening

OpenClaw connects the devices and services around the house through Home Assistant, giving it one place to monitor doors, locks, lights, water, climate, cars, sprinklers, network health, and the pool.

  • » Alerts when something needs attention, such as an unlocked door, a possible leak, a device going offline, or sprinklers failing to run
  • » Lovelace dashboards designed for quick household lookup and displayed on TRMNL e-ink screens around the house
  • » Rachio monitoring that verifies watering and automatically accounts for rain
  • » A pool chemistry agent that turns test readings into clear chemical doses and mixing instructions

Impact: Instead of checking several apps — or discovering problems after the fact — the house provides one reliable view and speaks up when something is wrong.

Life Automation

03

Less personal admin and fewer dropped balls

A collection of small automations that remove repetitive work from everyday life:

  • » Send a meal photo and get calories, macros, and a running daily food log
  • » Manage shared Costco and Walmart lists through chat, then push the Costco list to a reMarkable before shopping
  • » Send Todoist tasks to the reMarkable, then use handwritten checkmarks to complete them in Todoist
  • » Preview and remove unwanted email while preserving a veto window
  • » Turn school emails into a simple weekly digest, then create only the calendar events I approve

Impact: Routine tasks are captured where they naturally happen — chat, email, photos, or paper — and carried through without another app or dashboard to manage.

Music

04

Turn a DJ set into a Beatport playlist

Give OpenClaw the name or link of a DJ set. It finds the tracklist across multiple sources, matches the tracks against Beatport, and presents the results for review. After approval, it creates or updates the playlist while avoiding duplicates.

Impact: Rebuilding a set that used to require searching and matching tracks one by one becomes a quick review-and-approve workflow.
